--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Codebook for Activity 1 data 
Files counties_data_1.csv and counties_data_2.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
All data were downloaded from the 2015-2019 American Community Survey 
Units of observations: Counties in 2010 boundaries
 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Data Dictionary
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
GEOID: County ID

cy: County name

st: State name

medincome: Median household income 

rent: Median gross rent

whmedinc: White median household income 

blkmedinc: Black median household income

asnmedinc: Asian median household income

hispmedinc: Hispanic median household income 

tpop: Total population

white: Total white population

black: Total Black population

asian: Total Asian population	

hisp: Total Hispanic population
 
povdenom: Population for whom poverty status is determined

povnum: Population whose income in the past 12 months is below the poverty level
